FreePD (freepd.com), the first choice, closed in 2025 and could not be used.

Ogg Vorbis is not played by iOS, macOS or Windows system decoders, so the
engine uses these tracks on Android, Linux and the web and falls back to the
synthesised clips below elsewhere (or when a track fails to load).

## Synthesised clips (project-original)

Every `*.wav` in this folder is generated by `tool/make_sounds.py` from
simple oscillator, noise and filter models. They contain no third-party
material and are part of the project.

This covers the pen-on-pen hit clips (`hit_plastic_plastic_*`,
`hit_plastic_metal_*`, `hit_metal_metal_*`, `hit_soft_soft*`), built from
noise bursts, damped inharmonic partials and a low knock, and the soft
felt-marimba and pad stings (`victory`, `defeat`, `round_win`, `round_lose`).
All of it is our own synthesis.
