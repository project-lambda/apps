Framely is made by Abdullah Emroze. This policy explains what happens to your
information when you use the Framely app.

## The short version

Framely does not collect, store or share your personal data. There is no
account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## Photos and screenshots

When you add a screenshot, you choose it with your device's photo picker.
Framely can only see the images you pick. They are processed entirely on your
device to build your designs, and they are never uploaded to us or anyone
else.

Your projects are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They
are deleted when you delete a project or uninstall the app.

## Exports

When you export, the finished images are saved to your photo library or
handed to the app you choose in the share sheet. What happens after that is
governed by that app's own policy.

## Network use

Framely works entirely offline. Its typefaces ship inside the app, and it
makes no network requests.

## Children

Framely is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
information from anyone.
