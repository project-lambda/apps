Pen Fight is the desk game from school: flick your pen into the others and knock them off the edge before they knock you off.

Pull back on your pen like a slingshot and let go. Grab it near the end to spin it, near the middle to hit straight. Every pen weighs, grips and spins differently, and every desk slides differently, so the shot that wins on wood skids off glass.

Play a quick duel against a bot, climb the tournament ladder, or put up to four players around one screen. No sign-in, no ads, nothing to install.
