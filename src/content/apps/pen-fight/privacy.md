Pen Fight is made by Abdullah Emroze. This policy explains what happens to
your information when you play Pen Fight.

## The short version

Pen Fight does not collect your personal data. There is no account, no
analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## What Pen Fight stores

Your progress is stored on your device: your player name, coins, the pens and
desks you have unlocked, your tournament ladder and your settings. In the
browser version this lives in your browser's storage for this site; clear the
site's data to remove it. It is never sent anywhere.

## Network use

The browser version is served from this website, emroze.dev, which is hosted on
GitHub Pages. Loading it reveals your IP address to GitHub, as loading any web
page does. To draw a few characters its own fonts do not include, such as
emoji, the game may also load a font file from Google Fonts, which tells Google
your IP address. Neither request contains anything about you or your games.

Pen Fight makes no other network requests while you play.

## Children

Pen Fight is a game anyone can play. It collects no personal information from
anyone, including children under 13.
