DocScanner is made by Abdullah Emroze. This policy explains what happens to
your information when you use DocScanner.

## The short version

DocScanner does not collect, store or share your personal data. There is no
account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## Your scans

Scans are created on your device and saved as PDFs in the app's private
storage. They are never uploaded. They leave your device only when you share
one yourself, and are deleted when you delete them or uninstall the app.

## Camera

On iPhone, DocScanner asks for camera access so the scanner can see the page.
On Android, scanning runs in the system scanner provided by Google Play
Services, so the app itself does not need camera access. The camera is used
only while you are scanning.

## Network use

DocScanner works offline and makes no network requests of its own.

## Children

DocScanner is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
information from anyone.
