DocScanner turns paper into clean, straightened PDFs using the scanner that is already built into your phone.

Scans go into a library on your device, where you can share a PDF with any app or delete it for good. There is no account, no cloud storage and no advertising.

It is in development, and this page will get screenshots and a store link when it is closer to release.
