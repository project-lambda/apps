PhotoRelay is made by Abdullah Emroze. This policy explains what happens to
your information when you use PhotoRelay.

## The short version

PhotoRelay has no account and no servers. Your photos, videos and credentials
never reach us. There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## Your photos and videos

PhotoRelay reads the photos and videos you choose, on your device, and sends
them to the destinations you set up. Those destinations are yours: your
computer, your NAS, your cloud storage account, or another device running
PhotoRelay. Transfers go directly from your device to them.

We never receive your files, and we cannot see what you transfer or where you
send it. What happens to files after they arrive is governed by the policy of
the service or the device that holds them.

## Server details and passwords

Addresses, usernames, passwords, keys and tokens for your destinations are
stored in your device's secure storage, the keychain. They are used only to
connect to the servers you set up, and they are deleted when you remove the
destination or uninstall the app.

## On your local network

To find other devices, PhotoRelay announces itself on your local network with
the device name you choose. When receiving is switched on, it runs a small web
server on your device so another device or a browser on your network can send
files to it. Both are off unless you turn them on, and neither reaches the
internet.

## Transfer history

A record of what has been sent, and to where, is kept on your device so that
repeat transfers can skip files already sent. It stays on the device.

## Permissions

PhotoRelay asks for access to your photo library so it can transfer your
photos and videos, and for local network access so it can find your devices
and servers. On Mac, full disk access is optional and only needed to read or
write folders outside your home folder.

## Children

PhotoRelay is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
information from anyone.
